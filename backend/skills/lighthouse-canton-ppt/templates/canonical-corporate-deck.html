Integrated Wealth Management

Integrated Wealth Management

Portfolio construction and planning: Client Goal Alignment & Development → Portfolio Management → Client Progress Tracking.

Client Objectives

  • • Understand liquidity & capital situation and return expectations.
  • • Define investment goals, acceptable investments, portfolio limitations.
  • • Identify responsible parties & authorised banks/brokers.
  • • Identify and schedule anticipated cash outlay.

Asset Allocation

  • • Establish investment parameters.
  • • Utilize internal capital market assumptions to develop asset mix.
  • • Adjust asset allocation based on portfolio maturity, short- & long-term growth and risk objectives.

Security Selection

  • • Identify value creation opportunities amongst existing and new holdings.
  • • Create portfolio utilizing existing security pool.
  • • Timely and cost effective execution.

Risk Management

  • • Run risk analysis on asset classes for parameters like VaR, volatility, drawdowns.
  • • Apprise client of potential downsides in current asset mix and possible mitigations.

Optimisation

  • • Optimisation of current allocations to reach client goals and objectives.
  • • Overview of asset allocation to reduce asset class, sector and geographical exposures.

Benchmarking & Reporting

  • • Select appropriate Index / benchmark.
  • • Conduct periodic performance monitoring.
  • • Provide full compliance reporting.
  • • Provide monthly reporting and NAV / risk update.

Business Principles

Business Principles

Dimension Lighthouse Canton Traditional Wealth Managers
Business Model Mostly flat fee. Predominantly transaction / commission based.
Portfolio Construction Selection of assets primarily based on valuations. Predominantly bonds, equities & structured products.
Allocation Asset class / geography agnostic. Fixed allocation to asset classes and geographies.

04 · Investment Capabilities
Our Investment Philosophy

The 10 rules that guide our investment framework

01
Align Portfolios with Client InterestsEnsure investment strategies are in full harmony with the client's financial goals and risk tolerance.
02
Flexibility Across StrategiesBroad spectrum of investment avenues — stocks, bonds, long/short strategies, trade finance, relative value, etc. — to optimize risk-adjusted returns.
03
Dynamic RebalancingAdjust portfolios only when necessary, staying responsive to market conditions.
04
Prioritize Capital ProtectionEstablish clear boundaries for drawdowns before constructing portfolios, emphasizing safety of capital.
05
Work with Top-Tier Managers OnlySelect managers with proven track records, emphasizing high Sharpe, Calmar, and Sortino ratios.
06
DiversificationIncrease diversification in higher-risk investments (emerging market, high-yield, distressed) using well-diversified funds.
07
Leverage Earnings CompoundingFocus on the long-term compounding potential of earnings when choosing stocks.
08
Emphasize Cost EfficiencyMinimize expenses related to fund fees and transaction costs to maximize returns.
09
Research-Driven Asset AllocationEmploy a methodology that integrates valuations, cycles, and technical analysis.
10
Clear Exit Plan on Tactical TradesImplement tactical trades with predefined take-profit and stop-loss parameters to manage risk.

04 · Investment Capabilities
Coverage

Asset Class and Instrument Agnostic

Broad coverage with no inherent biases.
Equities
Capital Accumulation

Equities serve as a building block in many portfolios and are often considered a good way to benefit from long-term capital appreciation. Equities can have significant volatility and are suitable only for risk-tolerant investors.

Stocks · Options · Long/Short · Private Equity · etc.
Fixed Income
Capital Preservation &/or Yield Enhancement

Fixed income serves the needs of investors who require a steady stream of income to their portfolios. Considered less risky than equities, bonds can play an important role for more conservative investors.

Bonds · Private Debt · Long/Short · Alternative Lenders · Mortgages · etc.
Alternatives
Capital Accumulation &/or Yield Enhancement

Alternative investments provide returns driven by factors materially different from those of traditional asset classes such as bonds and equities. Alternatives are key to enabling the construction of truly well-balanced and diversified portfolios.

Real Estate · Macro · Insurance · Trade Finance · Commodities · Litigation · etc.
